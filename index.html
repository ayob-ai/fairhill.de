About

fairhill is a small management company in Berlin. We work with a few people and we stay with them.

We take care of the deals, tours, releases and rights, so you can spend your time making music.

Contracts get explained before you sign. Decisions are made with you, not for you. Fair terms are where we start, not a favour.

What we do

Career strategy

Where you want to be in a year, and in five. We work out the releases, collaborations and income that get you there.

Touring & booking

We work with agents and promoters to build routes that make sense for you and your budget.

Releases & labels

Deals, release plans, distribution. We deal with labels, distributors and publishers so you don't have to.

Producer representation

Placements, sessions, credits and splits. Producers and engineers get paid and named properly.

Partnerships & sync

Brand, film and game deals that fit the artist. We negotiate them, and they pay.
